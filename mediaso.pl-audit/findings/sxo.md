# SXO Analysis — mediaso.pl

Site: marketing agency for Polish medical facilities (kliniki, przychodnie, gabinety lekarskie i stomatologiczne).
Pages analyzed: `/` (366 words), `/uslugi` (380 words, 6 services on one page), `/o-nas` (116), `/kontakt` (44), `/portfolio` (30, empty), `/diagnostyka` (688, 20-question self-assessment quiz).

## Lead Finding: Structural Page-Type Mismatch

mediaso.pl runs on **5 pages total**, one of which is an empty placeholder. Every competitor that ranks for the queries this business needs to win runs a **multi-page architecture**: one dedicated page per service (pozycjonowanie, strony www, marketing stomatologiczny, social media) plus a blog/knowledge base for the informational layer. mediaso.pl compresses six services onto a single 380-word `/uslugi` page and has zero blog or article content. This is not a copy problem — it's an information-architecture problem, and it is the primary reason the site can't compete for the keyword set below.

## User Stories

1. As an **owner of a multi-doctor przychodnia comparing agencies** ("Top 5 najlepszych agencji marketingowych dla placówek medycznych" ranking #3 for the head term), I want to see a shortlist-style comparison of what makes one agency different from another, because I'm choosing between several similar-sounding offers, but I'm blocked by **comparison fatigue** — mediaso.pl's `/uslugi` page lists features, not differentiators, and `/portfolio` has nothing to compare against.
   *(Source: agencjawroclawska.pl listicle ranking #3 for "marketing dla placówek medycznych")*

2. As a **stomatolog researching gabinet-specific marketing** (query "marketing dla gabinetu stomatologicznego" dominated by niche-dedicated pages/domains), I want a page that speaks directly to dental-practice concerns, because generic medical marketing copy doesn't address my patient-acquisition specifics (implanty, higienistki, NFZ vs. prywatne), but I'm blocked by **information gap** — mediaso.pl mentions "gabinetów lekarskich, stomatologicznych" only in passing on the homepage subhead and nowhere else.
   *(Source: 4/6 top results for this query are niche-dedicated pages/domains built around dentistry specifically)*

3. As a **skeptical placówka manager evaluating trust before contacting an agency** (SERP consensus that winning service pages show named realizations, e.g. intracom.pl's "nasze-realizacje" ranking #2 for "strony internetowe dla przychodni"), I want to see real client names, before/after examples or numbers, because I'm about to hand over my clinic's reputation to an outside vendor, but I'm blocked by a **trust gap** — mediaso's `/portfolio` (30 words) states case studies are "w przygotowaniu."
   *(Source: intracom.pl #2 ranking driven by named realizations; taxonomy requires case studies for Service Page type)*

4. As an **early-stage researcher typing a broad definitional query** ("marketing medyczny," dominated 67% by blog/glossary content), I want to understand what medical marketing actually involves before I'm ready to talk to a vendor, because I'm still forming my strategy and not ready to buy, but I'm blocked by a **content gap** — mediaso.pl has no blog, glossary, or educational article anywhere on the site, so it cannot appear for this stage of the journey at all.
   *(Source: 4/6 top results for "marketing medyczny" are blog/knowledge-base articles)*

5. As a **placówka owner trying to self-diagnose before committing budget** (no competing interactive tool appears in any of the 5 sampled SERPs), I want a low-commitment way to assess my own marketing gaps, because I'm not ready for a sales call yet, but I'm blocked by **low visibility** — the `/diagnostyka` quiz that would serve this exact need is not surfaced by any target keyword and sits one nav click deep with no supporting content driving traffic to it.
   *(Source: absence of any Tool/Interactive result across all 5 SERPs sampled; taxonomy notes Tool pages face minimal competition when present)*

Stories span awareness (4, 5), consideration (1, 2), and decision (3) stages.

### Weakest Persona: Early-stage researcher (22/100)

**Top issue:** No content exists anywhere on the site for this journey stage — no blog, no glossary, no educational article. This persona cannot find mediaso.pl through Google at all for definitional queries.

**Recommended fix:** Publish a knowledge-base section (`/wiedza/` or `/blog/`) starting with the exact queries where blogs dominate: "czym jest marketing medyczny," "pozycjonowanie kliniki krok po kroku." Each article should link into the relevant `/uslugi` split-page (see below) and offer the `/diagnostyka` quiz as a secondary CTA.

### Second Weakest: Stomatolog persona (31/100)

**Top issue:** Zero dedicated content for the dental vertical despite it being named as a target audience.

**Recommended fix:** Split a `/marketing-stomatologiczny` page out of `/uslugi`, matching the pattern of 2heads.agency and dtmarketing.pl (both ranking on dedicated dental-vertical pages/domains). Include dental-specific pain points (higienistki, NFZ vs. prywatne, implanty) rather than generic medical copy.

### Systemic Issue Across All Personas

**Trust dimension averages 5.6/25** — the lowest of all four dimensions across every persona. Root cause: `/portfolio` is an empty placeholder and no page on the site carries a named client, a number ("+40% zapytań od pacjentów"), or a testimonial.

### Priority Actions

1. Populate `/portfolio` with at least 2-3 named case studies (client name, before/after metric, service used) — fixes the single lowest-scoring dimension across every persona.
2. Split `/uslugi` into dedicated pages per service, starting with the two highest-intent ones from the SERP set: `pozycjonowanie klinik` and `marketing stomatologiczny` — each needs its own URL, 500+ words, process section, and CTA.
3. Launch a knowledge-base/blog section targeting the definitional queries ("marketing medyczny," "pozycjonowanie kliniki") where Google rewards Blog Post format ~67% of the time — this also builds the topical authority a YMYL-adjacent niche (healthcare) requires.
4. Surface `/diagnostyka` as a top-level CTA from the homepage and future blog posts — it's the one asset with zero direct competition in the sampled SERPs and is currently under one nav click with no inbound content driving to it.

## Limitations

- SERP analysis used WebSearch (not a rank-tracking API), so exact ranking positions, PAA box contents, ads, and AI Overview presence could not be directly captured — page-type classification is based on the organic result set returned, not a full SERP feature audit.
- Only 5-7 organic results were available per query from WebSearch output (not always a full top 10), so consensus percentages are directional, not statistically precise.
- Local pack / Google Business Profile presence for these queries was not assessed — given "pozycjonowanie klinik" surfaced local-SEO framing repeatedly, a dedicated `/seo local` GBP analysis would sharpen this further.
- Competitor page word counts and structure were estimated from search snippets and titles, not full page renders — depth comparisons are directional.
- Diagnostyka quiz's actual completion/conversion behavior (client-side interactivity) was not tested; SPA rendering was not required since the site returned `mode_used: raw` (no JS-gated content detected).

## Cross-Skill Recommendations

- Missing case studies and named client trust signals → run `/seo content` for E-E-A-T-focused content strategy on `/portfolio` and `/o-nas`.
- OfferCatalog/Service schema is present but Review/AggregateRating and FAQPage are missing → run `/seo schema` to generate the missing schema types once case studies and FAQ content exist.
- "pozycjonowanie klinik" and "marketing dla gabinetu stomatologicznego" SERPs show strong local-SEO framing (dentist + city patterns) → run `/seo local` to check whether mediaso.pl's own Google Business Profile and any location-specific content support local intent, separate from the client-facing service pages.
- `/uslugi` thin bundled-page problem → run `/seo page` for a page-level audit once the split into dedicated service pages is planned, to size word count and structure targets per new page.
